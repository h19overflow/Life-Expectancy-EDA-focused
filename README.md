Title: EDA and Statistical Analysis of Factors Influencing Global Life Expectancy (WHO)
Tech: Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, SciPy.stats
Conducted in-depth EDA on WHO dataset (~2900 rows, 22 features) covering 193 countries (2000-2015) to understand determinants of life expectancy.
Generated insightful visualizations using Seaborn/Matplotlib, revealing strong positive associations between Life Expectancy and factors like Schooling and GDP, while highlighting differences across regions.
Performed hypothesis testing (t-tests, Pearson correlation) to statistically confirm key findings, such as the significant impact of healthcare expenditure and significant differences in life expectancy between 'Developed' and 'Developing' nations (p < 0.001).
Synthesized findings into a detailed Jupyter Notebook, outlining actionable insights on health and socioeconomic indicators related to longevity.
